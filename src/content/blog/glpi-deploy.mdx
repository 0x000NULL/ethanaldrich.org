GLPI is the ITSM platform behind support and asset management for the two
companies I run technology for. The useful thing to write about is not the
install — that part is an afternoon — but what changed once there was a single
place where requests and assets lived.

## Why not just a shared inbox

Support had been arriving by phone, text, and by people finding me in a hallway.
That works until it doesn't, and the failure is not that things get dropped. The
failure is that you cannot answer basic questions: what breaks most often, which
site consumes the most support, how long anything actually takes. None of that is
recoverable from memory across eight locations.

A ticket queue is the visible part. The asset inventory is the part that paid for
itself.

## Categories, SLAs and the ITIL part

ITIL v4 gives this its vocabulary: incident versus problem versus change versus
service request. The distinction sounds like bureaucracy until you have a
recurring incident and no way to express that the *cause* is one open problem
rather than fifteen separate tickets.
