## The proving ground

A multi-node Proxmox VE cluster running dozens of self-hosted services, behind
Nginx and reachable over Tailscale. It has been running since 2020.

Its real function is not the services. It is that almost everything I have put
into production at work was broken here first, on hardware I own, where the
failure costs nothing but an evening.

## What it runs

- **Proxmox VE** across multiple nodes, mixing full VMs and LXC containers
  depending on what the workload actually needs
- **Docker** for the service tier
- **Nginx** as reverse proxy
- **Tailscale** for access, so nothing needs to be published to the open internet
  to be reachable

## Why it earns its keep

Three specific things moved from here into production:

**Container and VM boundaries.** Learning where LXC is sufficient and where a
full VM is the honest answer is much cheaper to learn on your own cluster than
during a migration.

**Reverse proxy and TLS termination.** The Nginx patterns now in front of the
Budget application stack were worked out here first.

**Network segmentation.** Running genuinely untrusted workloads next to things I
care about, on one set of hardware, is a useful forcing function. The same
instinct shows up in the VLAN segmentation at work and on the yacht builds.

It is also where the security tooling gets evaluated before it goes anywhere
near a cardholder data environment. Reading a vendor comparison tells you what a
tool claims. Running it for a month tells you what it costs to operate.
